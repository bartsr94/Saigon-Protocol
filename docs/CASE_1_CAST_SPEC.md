# Case 1 Cast Spec

*Working cast spec for the Aveline breakout investigation. This document translates the current story direction into named characters, district ties, clue ownership, and asset needs.*

## Goal

Define the core characters required to make Case 1 function as an investigation rather than just a premise.

This spec is focused on:

- who the key players are
- what each knows
- what each is hiding
- where they belong in the city
- how they feed the breadcrumb structure
- what art / content support they require

The cast here is intentionally modest in size. Each character should matter, recur, and reveal a distinct kind of compromise or pressure rather than blending into a large ensemble.

## Cast design principles

- Keep the story noir first: everyone should be understandable before they are condemnable.
- Keep the horror systemic: the cast reveals institutional evil through choices, paperwork, fear, and silence.
- Avoid redundancy: each major character should own a different angle on the truth.
- Tie people to districts: the city should feel populated by where people live and operate, not just by plot function.
- Let some characters be useful outside the main case: side stories and optional encounters should deepen their relationships.

## Aveline internal cast

### Mei Hong

- **Role:** District 4 lab operations lead
- **District tie:** District 4
- **Function in story:** first point of contact, controlled guide, partial liar, early emotional anchor
- **Public presentation:** poised, tired, competent, running on discipline more than sleep
- **Core truth:** she has spent too long turning moral catastrophe into manageable procedure

#### What Mei Hong knows

- HN-12 was alive, unstable, and still mentally present
- the break-in story is false
- the inner lab contains evidence that will break Aveline's official version
- internal cleanup is already underway the moment CID arrives

#### What Mei Hong is hiding

- how long she knew the project had crossed the line
- whether she deliberately helped create the opening for official police involvement
- how much personal contact she had with HN-12

#### Story use

- appears at the opening scene
- withholds key truth in first interview
- becomes more useful once the detective proves they cannot be easily managed
- may become a reluctant ally or a morally compromised witness depending on player handling

#### Clue ownership

- first cracks in the burglary story
- practical details about the lab's routines and containment protocols
- emotional evidence that HN-12 was not treated as fully non-person

#### Asset needs

- existing portrait remains usable for now
- potential second background or scene art variant for inner-lab conversations later

## Dr. Lucien Vale

- **Role:** principal investigator / project director
- **District tie:** District 4 professionally, District 1 socially / institutionally
- **Function in story:** ideological antagonist, voice of rationalized necessity
- **Public presentation:** calm, articulate, grave, almost painfully reasonable
- **Core truth:** he believes the work is too important to stop, even after the deaths

#### What Vale knows

- the full HN-1 through HN-12 timeline
- the real purpose of the project
- why Saigon and Nusantara-linked intake made the project viable
- which failures were accepted because the data remained promising

#### What Vale is hiding

- how early Aveline understood the instability pattern
- the extent to which subjects ceased being patients and became expendable iterations
- which internal reports recommended pausing or terminating the line

#### Story use

- should not appear too early or too often
- works best as a later interview once the player has enough knowledge to challenge him
- may remain maddeningly composed even while the case collapses around him

#### Clue ownership

- ideological logic of the project
- internal framing of adaptation as historical necessity
- confirmation that the project was not a rogue lab but an institutional decision

#### Asset needs

- new portrait
- possible District 1 or executive-interior background support

## Sora Baek

- **Role:** corporate security liaison
- **District tie:** District 4 operationally, District 1 for corporate coordination
- **Function in story:** access gatekeeper, pressure source, face of active management
- **Public presentation:** professional, severe, efficient, never visibly flustered
- **Core truth:** truth only matters to her after scandal containment

#### What Sora knows

- there was no break-in
- Aveline recovery teams are searching for a living subject
- internal orders differ on whether HN-12 should be recovered alive
- which witnesses are being managed or isolated

#### What Sora is hiding

- the real scope of the search effort
- how much scene staging occurred after the breach
- whether she has already suppressed evidence or personnel

#### Story use

- recurring obstruction character
- appears whenever access is controlled, delayed, or narrowed
- can pressure the detective without ever needing theatrical villain energy

#### Clue ownership

- timeline inconsistencies around site lockdown
- proof that multiple search teams were deployed under different justifications
- confirmation that Aveline is afraid of something far more urgent than stolen research

#### Asset needs

- new portrait
- no special personal background required at first

## Elian Morrow

- **Role:** compliance and licensing officer
- **District tie:** District 1
- **Function in story:** paper-trail antagonist, avatar of administrative evil
- **Public presentation:** bloodless, formal, exacting, offended by imprecision
- **Core truth:** if a thing can be correctly classified, he believes it can be morally absorbed

#### What Morrow knows

- the subject classification scheme
- how waivers and emergency-adaptation protocols were structured
- how transit deaths and identity laundering were made survivable on paper
- what formal relationship existed between Aveline and Nusantara channels

#### What Morrow is hiding

- how deniability was intentionally engineered into the paperwork
- what internal category HN-12 was treated as once personhood became inconvenient
- which legal documents were written to survive inquiry rather than describe reality

#### Story use

- best used in District 1 scenes
- more chilling if the player reaches him through legitimate records channels rather than a dramatic confrontation
- should frustrate by being technically careful, not slippery

#### Clue ownership

- ghost identities
- emergency research authorizations
- trail from refugee intake to medical classification

#### Asset needs

- new portrait
- District 1 bureaucratic / records background support

## Nhi Quyen

- **Role:** junior researcher or subject-care technician
- **District tie:** District 4 primarily, possibly District 5 by family / living ties
- **Function in story:** missing witness, conscience of the lab, human bridge to HN-12
- **Public presentation:** mostly absent at first; known through traces before direct appearance
- **Core truth:** she could no longer tolerate what was being done and tried to act

#### What Nhi knows

- HN-12's real name
- the subject's lucid behavior and fear
- practical details of their day-to-day suffering
- what event immediately preceded the breach

#### What Nhi is hiding

- whether she tried to free HN-12
- whether she was documenting the abuse
- whether she stole or copied files before disappearing

#### Story use

- should function as a ghost in the investigation before becoming a full character
- ideal breadcrumb source through logs, mentions, missing-shift notes, and witness recollections
- direct encounter should matter emotionally

#### Clue ownership

- HN-12's humanity
- the difference between official subject language and lived reality
- the most intimate evidence of what the project did to a person

#### Asset needs

- new portrait
- possible District 5 or safe-flat background if she survives into a late meeting

## Tomas Varga

- **Role:** containment / systems engineer
- **District tie:** District 4 for work, likely lower-status district residence elsewhere
- **Function in story:** technical witness, forensics support, compromised skilled labor
- **Public presentation:** defensive, tired, prickly, not paid enough for what he has seen
- **Core truth:** he knew the infrastructure was being pushed beyond safe tolerance

#### What Tomas knows

- the breach pattern does not support outside intrusion
- which containment systems had been modified or overstressed
- how often emergency patches replaced real fixes
- what signs indicated the subject remained physically dangerous and cognitively active

#### What Tomas is hiding

- that he signed off on unsafe changes
- that he accepted false framing about the subject's level of awareness
- how much fear, guilt, or self-protection drives his current silence

#### Story use

- useful as the person who lets the detective prove breakout, not break-in
- best encountered outside the lab after he has had time to panic and consider disappearing

#### Clue ownership

- physical mechanics of the breach
- proof that evidence was staged after the event
- difference between damage made by escape and damage made by forced entry

#### Asset needs

- new portrait

## Narrative relationship triangle

The emotional center of the Aveline side of the case should sit between:

- **Mei Hong:** "I let this happen too long."
- **Lucien Vale:** "It still had to happen."
- **Nhi Quyen:** "It never should have happened at all."

This triangle gives the player three different moral relationships to the same project:

- procedural complicity
- ideological conviction
- failed resistance

## Distinct kinds of guilt

Each internal Aveline character should represent a different mode of institutional harm:

- **Mei Hong:** moral exhaustion
- **Lucien Vale:** necessity as ideology
- **Sora Baek:** control and suppression
- **Elian Morrow:** administrative laundering
- **Nhi Quyen:** conscience under pressure
- **Tomas Varga:** technical complicity

This is important. The player should feel they are uncovering a structure, not just six bad personalities.

## District distribution

### District 4

- Mei Hong
- Lucien Vale
- Sora Baek
- Tomas Varga
- Nhi Quyen's traces

### District 1

- Lucien Vale in formal / executive contexts
- Sora Baek when coordinating above the lab level
- Elian Morrow

### District 5

- possible off-hours or hidden connections for Nhi
- former employee testimony that contextualizes Aveline's internal cast

## Clue ladder

This cast should support a gradual reveal sequence:

1. **Mei Hong** introduces the controlled lie.
2. **Sora Baek** enforces the sealed perimeter and makes the player feel managed.
3. **Former witnesses and city contacts** expose missing context and missing people.
4. **Tomas Varga** destabilizes the burglary fiction with engineering truth.
5. **Elian Morrow** reveals how the paperwork protected the project.
6. **Lucien Vale** reveals why the project existed and why the deaths were tolerated.
7. **Nhi Quyen** or her trace material restores HN-12's humanity.

This order can flex, but the overall flow should move from suspicion to system to personhood.

## Writing guardrails

- Do not let Aveline employees collapse into interchangeable "corp people."
- Let at least some of them make arguments that are partly true.
- Avoid melodrama in formal scenes; cold language is often more disturbing.
- Keep every secret tied to fear, ambition, guilt, or institutional loyalty.
- Preserve the sense that everyone involved has been living with this longer than the detective has.

## Open questions

- What exact specialty does Mei Hong own: operations, subject care, or lab logistics?
- Is Nhi alive and hiding, captured, or dead by the time the detective is close?
- How public-facing is Lucien Vale inside Aveline's District 4 footprint?
- Does Sora Baek report primarily into Aveline, Baekho-linked security channels, or a mixed chain?
- What is Tomas Varga's employment status after the breach: active, suspended, disappeared, or on the run?

## Recommendation

Use this cast as the fixed internal Aveline spine for Case 1.

Do not expand the employee roster further until we write the district-by-district breadcrumb chain. If more people are added too early, the investigation risks losing clarity before the city-facing supporting cast is even defined.
