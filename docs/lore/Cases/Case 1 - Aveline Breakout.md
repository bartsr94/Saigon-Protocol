# Case 1 - Aveline Breakout

*Draft story bible for the opening investigation. Working document, not settled canon.*

## Core premise

The player detective is called to an [[Aveline Biogenetics]] laboratory in District 4 for what is officially described as a break-in and theft of proprietary research material. This alone is suspicious: a firm like Aveline would normally keep a laboratory incident in-house through private security, insurance, and SEZAC-facing legal cover.

The real reason the [[Saigon Constabulary]] is being brought in is that Aveline needs time, reach, and plausible deniability. There was no break-in. A living experimental subject escaped containment. Aveline needs an official external story while its own teams search for the subject, clean internal records, and decide whether recovery or destruction is the safer option.

The detective's official case is to investigate a burglary. The real case is to discover that a corporation trying to solve an impossible climate problem crossed the line from emergency medicine into administratively tolerated human ruin.

This case should not play as a fast sprint from lab to culprit. It is the opening investigation and one of the player's first real ways of learning Saigon in 2226. The detective should follow a slow trail of contradictions, witnesses, favors, hostile institutions, and side encounters through multiple districts before the full truth comes into focus.

## Noir thesis

This story should stay noir first and horror second.

The horror does not come from a random monster, a secret superweapon, or cackling evil scientists. It comes from people under real pressure trying to do genuine good inside a system that rewards secrecy, expedience, and moral compartmentalization. The evil is procedural before it is theatrical: waivers, exceptions, ghost files, reclassified persons, emergency authorizations, and the quiet belief that one more compromise is justified if the work might save millions later.

The escaped subject is not "the monster of the week." They are the truth Aveline can no longer keep abstract.

## Official story vs. real story

### What CID is told

- Unknown intruder entered the laboratory overnight.
- One highly sensitive research asset was stolen.
- Several internal systems were damaged during the theft.
- Aveline is cooperating, but some areas remain restricted due to contamination and IP controls.

### What actually happened

- Subject HN-12 escaped from an internal containment suite.
- A containment breach killed or maimed at least one member of staff.
- Aveline security altered the scene to resemble outside intrusion.
- The "stolen asset" story is cover for a missing living subject and missing support records.
- Multiple Aveline teams are now searching the city under different instructions.

## Aveline's actual research goal

The project is an attempt to push climate adaptation beyond standard licensed tolerance therapies. Existing heat-survival modifications keep people alive in a hothouse Earth. Aveline wants the next step: bodies that can remain functional in wet-bulb extremes, chemical haze, and flood-zone field conditions that still cripple or kill most people after prolonged exposure.

Publicly, the work can be justified as lifesaving medical advancement for laborers, first responders, flood-wall crews, salvage divers, and populations living in marginal environments. Privately, it has obvious value for security forces, industrial labor, off-book deployments, and any institution that benefits from people who can endure conditions others cannot.

This matters to the case: Aveline is not doing useless cruelty. It is pursuing a real and desperately needed solution. That is what makes the harm harder to dismiss and easier for its perpetrators to rationalize.

## Why this research is happening in Saigon

Saigon SEZ is the perfect place for Aveline to attempt this work.

- The zone is a regulatory frontier where ethics and oversight are weak if the results look useful enough.
- The city is a climate choke point, making adaptation research easy to justify as public good.
- Refugees and displaced laborers continue to move through Saigon on their way north or into new labor systems.
- The city's layered bureaucracy allows inconvenient people to disappear into paperwork gaps.

This should not read as Aveline choosing Saigon because it is lawless in a cartoon sense. It chose Saigon because the city is where climate desperation, logistical throughput, and corporate impunity overlap most profitably.

## Nusantara's role

Nusantara is tied to the case not only as muscle, but as pipeline.

- Some refugees entering the zone pass through Nusantara-administered intake, transit, housing, or medical referral systems.
- Aveline's research program gains access to legally fragile people who can be reclassified, rerouted, or quietly declared dead in transit.
- Not everyone inside Nusantara understands the full arrangement.
- Some officers know exactly what is happening and profit from it.
- Some rank and file believe they are moving people into legitimate adaptation or treatment channels.
- Some participants justify it because they still dream of one day making the drowned archipelago livable again.

That last point matters. Nusantara's involvement should be tragic as well as corrupt: part opportunism, part institutional drift, part sincere belief that if adaptation is the only road back to Indonesia or Malaysia, then someone has to walk it first.

## Subject HN-12

### Identity

HN-12 should begin the case as an opaque asset code, not a person the detective knows by name. One of the major emotional turns of the case is discovering that the "asset" had a real name, a real prior life, and enough lucidity to fear what was being done to them.

Working concept:

- Registered to the project under a heat-resilience clinical identifier rather than a civilian identity.
- A refugee from the drowned Indonesian / Malaysian archipelago corridor, routed through Nusantara-linked systems.
- Drawn from an undocumented or legally vulnerable population pool.
- Officially classed as a voluntary trial participant under emergency-adaptation protocols.
- In reality, their consent was murky, pressured, incomplete, or rendered meaningless by desperation.

### Biological outcome

HN-12 is not a giant bio-weapon. They are a human body pushed into unstable adaptation.

- Thermoregulation is radically altered.
- Skin, vasculature, and glandular response have changed in ways that read as visibly wrong up close.
- The subject can tolerate environmental extremes that should debilitate others.
- Their metabolism is unstable and requires chemical support or tightly managed conditions.
- Sensory processing and stress response have been disrupted, producing panic, pain, and periods of impaired judgment.

The body horror should be intimate and tragic: wrong temperature, wrong breathing, wrong texture, wrong movement, but still recognizably human.

### Psychological state

HN-12 is frightened, in pain, intermittently lucid, and increasingly desperate. They are not prowling the city as a slasher villain. They escaped because containment had become intolerable and because someone inside the lab either helped them flee or failed to stop them on purpose.

At least some collateral violence after the escape should read as the behavior of a terrified person in biological crisis, not pure predation.

One of the most disturbing side effects is intermittent psychosis. HN-12 does not remain in one stable mental state. In lucid stretches they can understand what was done to them and react with horror. In unstable stretches they misread threat, lose continuity, or act with frightening force and panic. The tragedy is that the moments of clarity may be just enough for them to appreciate what they have become.

## HN-1 through HN-11

HN-12 is not the first subject. Aveline's line is already paved with dead ends.

- Officially, HN-1 through HN-11 were inconclusive trials whose patients did not survive complications.
- Internally, each failure moved the threshold of what the project was willing to normalize.
- Some subjects died from treatment stress.
- Some became non-viable after severe instability.
- Some may have been euthanized once they could no longer produce useful data.

This history should be discovered gradually. At first it reads like ordinary clinical loss. Later it becomes clear that the project had a body count long before HN-12 escaped.

## What went wrong

The core failure is not merely "the experiment became unstable." The deeper failure is that instability was already known and administratively absorbed.

Possible chain:

1. Early trial signs showed severe metabolic volatility and psychological breakdown.
2. The project should have paused.
3. Internal pressure kept it alive because the data was too promising, the market too valuable, or the political stakes too high.
4. HN-12 deteriorated under continued testing.
5. A staff member attempted to help, intervene, or leak evidence.
6. Containment failed during or shortly after that intervention.

This lets the player discover not one mistake, but a staircase of rationalized decisions.

## Why Aveline uses official channels

Aveline does not call CID because it trusts the law. It calls because official process is temporarily useful.

- An external case number justifies visible police movement and perimeter activity.
- CID can ask questions in neighborhoods where Aveline's own search teams would draw more suspicion.
- The break-in narrative buys time while internal teams try to recover the subject and the records.
- Aveline can later claim civilian harm resulted from industrial theft or sabotage rather than unlawful human experimentation.

Most importantly, someone inside Aveline wants an outside witness on record before the entire incident can be buried.

## Internal Aveline factions

### Mei Hong

Mei Hong is the detective's first point of contact and the lab face the player already meets in `intro.ink`.

- Role: senior lab technician or operations lead for the District 4 site
- Core conflict: complicit in the work, but no longer convinced the people above her will draw any moral line at all
- Why she cooperates: bringing in CID makes total erasure harder
- What she withholds: the subject was alive, known to be unstable, and possibly still mentally present

She should feel suspicious for good reason, but not villainous in a simple way.

### Dr. Lucien Vale

- Role: project director
- Public face: grave, clinical, speaks the language of necessity and triage
- Real stance: believes the work is too important to stop, even now
- Priority: recover the subject and preserve the project

Vale is useful if we want the articulate "history will forgive this if it works" voice.

### Sora Baek

- Role: Aveline corporate security liaison
- Function: controls access, delays the detective, shapes the break-in narrative
- Priority: contain scandal first, facts second

Baek is who the player feels as obstruction before they understand the whole shape of the lie.

### Elian Morrow

- Role: compliance and licensing officer
- Function: keeper of the paper trail, waivers, and legal classifications
- Priority: prove every action was authorized enough to survive scrutiny

Morrow embodies administrative evil without needing to be physically threatening.

### Nhi Quyen

- Role: junior researcher or care technician
- Function: probable internal sympathizer who tried to help HN-12
- Status: missing, dead, disappeared, or being blamed as the "intruder's inside accomplice"

Nhi is the easiest route to the human truth of the project.

### Former employees

The case should also involve people no longer inside Aveline's walls.

- dismissed technicians
- burned-out support staff
- contractors who saw only fragments of the truth
- clinic workers who handled off-book follow-up

These characters let the detective build the case by social pressure and memory rather than lab access alone.

### Nusantara enforcer / facilitator

At least one recurring Nusantara-linked character should sit at the seam between transport, coercion, and belief.

- Role: enforcer, escort, or intake supervisor
- Public stance: did what was necessary to keep people moving and alive
- Hidden guilt: knows some "medical transfers" never came back
- Story value: can be menace, reluctant informant, or eventual witness depending on how the detective handles them

This figure is useful because they connect the refugee pipeline to the experiment without turning Nusantara itself into a monolith.

## District focus

The main investigation should be built around four districts:

- **District 4** - Aveline lab, flood-wall infrastructure, first public violence, immediate institutional pressure
- **District 1** - CID channels, SEZAC paperwork, upper-level corporate influence, official obstruction
- **District 5 / Cholon** - informants, gray clinics, forged papers, labor brokers, former employees hiding in dense civilian space
- **District 2** - sacrificed, half-drowned zone; final pursuit space and thematic endpoint

These four areas should feel distinct in class, atmosphere, and social texture. The player should get a lived sense of the city, not just a sequence of plot rooms.

## Open-world framing

The opening case is the spine of the main story, but the player should encounter Saigon as a city larger than the case.

- Side stories should exist both adjacent to and outside the investigation.
- Some characters met for optional reasons can later matter to the case.
- Some witnesses will only talk if the detective helps with unrelated local problems first.
- Some districts should offer atmosphere, errands, and recurring faces that deepen the sense of place even when they do not immediately advance the plot.

The investigation should therefore feel like it is happening inside a living city rather than pulling the player down a single corridor.

## Investigation spine

### Act 1 - The lie as procedure

The detective arrives at the lab and is given the burglary story. The scene is tightly managed. Some witnesses look rehearsed, some frightened, and some genuinely confused. The player should notice:

- damage that feels too selective
- unusual urgency around citywide search efforts
- staff more afraid of Aveline's internal response than of the supposed thief
- restricted access to the actual containment wing

The mood is suspicion, not open horror.

The player should not gain access to the inner lab at this stage. Aveline can plausibly stonewall behind contamination, proprietary controls, and ongoing internal review.

### Act 2 - The city's edges

While Aveline stalls access to the breach area, the detective follows the official burglary route out into the city. This is where the case opens outward and slows down into real detective work:

- transport records do not align
- trial participants appear under ghost identities
- clinic, labor, or housing channels overlap in troubling ways
- Aveline teams are clearly searching for something more urgent than stolen data
- former employees reveal fragments that do not fit the official story
- a Nusantara-linked contact suggests certain "deaths in transit" were administratively convenient

The player should begin to feel that the missing "asset" is a person.

### Act 3 - Public violence and leverage

At first HN-12 does not leave an obvious trail of murder. Early incidents can be read as unrelated:

- break-ins for food, water, or chemical supplies
- people injured while trying to intervene
- glimpses of a wrong-looking, panicked figure that produce contradictory witness statements

Then the case turns. A serious assault or murder in District 4 creates political pressure and damages the burglary fiction. Once the detective brings enough contradictions or public heat back to Aveline, the company loses the ability to keep every inner door shut.

This is the point where the player earns deeper access.

### Act 4 - Breakout, not break-in

Once access is finally granted to the sealed section, the physical scene breaks the official story.

- damage radiates outward from containment
- restraint points failed from the inside
- blood, tissue, and chemical residue track escape rather than intrusion
- outer evidence has been staged or retrofitted
- cleanup efforts are visible if the player looks closely enough

This is the major detective reveal: the room itself testifies against Aveline.

### Act 5 - The drowned city

The case becomes a race between CID, Aveline recovery teams, and anyone else trying to find HN-12. The detective now knows:

- HN-12 is alive or was alive after the breach
- Aveline is not trying only to recover property
- there may be a second missing figure connected to the escape
- every hour matters because the subject is physiologically degrading

The breadcrumb trail should now pull the player through Cholon and finally toward the sacrificed zones of District 2, where bodies like HN-12 were always meant to prove humanity could survive.

The mood shifts from suspicion into dread and pity.

### Act 6 - Lucid encounter

The climax should be a meeting with HN-12 in one of their lucid windows, ideally in District 2 amid the drowned and abandoned spaces the city gave up on.

This scene is where the case stops being abstract:

- HN-12 can speak, however haltingly
- the detective learns enough of the person beneath the project code
- the subject may understand what was done to them and ask for something human rather than institutional
- the lucid moment may collapse, forcing a terrible final choice

Possible end beats include mercy, surrender, breakdown into violence, or a compromised handover depending on how dark and how noir we want the opening case to land.

## Key revelations

- The break-in story was fabricated after the fact.
- The "stolen research asset" is a living human being.
- HN-12's official consent and legal status were fraudulent or coercive.
- Nusantara-linked transit and intake channels helped source subjects who could disappear on paper.
- HN-1 through HN-11 all died under a project Aveline kept advancing anyway.
- Aveline knew the project had crossed stability and ethics lines before the escape.
- Someone inside the lab tried to stop what was happening.
- Different Aveline actors want different outcomes: recovery, silence, destruction, or survivable blame.

## Possible ending shapes

### Recovery ending

HN-12 is returned to Aveline alive. This is the cleanest official resolution and likely the dirtiest moral one unless the detective extracts some concession or exposure along the way.

### Exposure ending

The detective gets enough proof into the hands of the Constabulary, press, or a rival power to make burial impossible. This may not save HN-12, but it forces the city to look at what Aveline was doing.

### Mercy ending

HN-12 is found in a terminal, lucid, or semi-lucid state and the detective chooses a path centered on the subject's humanity rather than the institution's claims. This could mean protecting them briefly, helping them disappear, or ending their suffering depending on how dark we want the first case to go.

### Compromised ending

The detective learns the truth but accepts a partial cover-up in exchange for saving some smaller set of lives, shielding a witness, or preventing worse corporate retaliation. This is probably the most noir resolution.

## Tone guardrails

- Keep the body horror personal, not bombastic.
- Keep the subject human in the player's eyes even when they are dangerous.
- Let Aveline's people make sincere arguments that are not wholly false.
- Let the player spend real time in the city before the full horror resolves into view.
- Avoid turning the case into pure action-thriller pacing too early.
- Let procedural clues carry as much weight as shocking visuals.
- Make every escalation feel like one more layer of tolerated compromise being uncovered.

## Questions to refine next

- Was Nhi Quyen helping the subject escape, or trying to document the abuse, or both?
- How much did Mei Hong know, and when did she decide official channels were the lesser evil?
- Which Compact-aligned actor benefits from Aveline being exposed, and which one wants it buried?
- Who is the key Nusantara-linked intermediary the detective can pressure, befriend, or break?
- What are the exact breadcrumb scenes in Districts 1, 4, 5, and 2?
- What exact physiological signs make HN-12 horrifying, memorable, and still tragically human?
