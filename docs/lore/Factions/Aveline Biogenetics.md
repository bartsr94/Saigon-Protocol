# Aveline Biogenetics

**Type:** Biotech firm **Origin:** EU-linked **Role:** Minority stakeholder in [[SEZAC]] **Thematic link:** Genetic adaptation / modification arc

## Overview

Aveline is one of the "others with their fingers here" let into Saigon SEZ under the old Vietnamese government's last-ditch invitation to anyone willing to help. Where [[Kaien Sogo]] and [[Baekho Group]] compete over infrastructure, logistics, and territory, Aveline's stake is narrower and stranger: it's plausibly among the entities that pioneered — or now license — the genetic-adaptation therapies underlying humanity's survival on a hothouse Earth, from the earliest sickle-cell-style gene edits through to fully custom genomes.

## Function in Play

Any case touching genetic modification, a gray-market gene clinic, or unlicensed body mods has a real chance of leading back to Aveline's name — whether the company wants the attention or not. Aveline is a good source of morally ambiguous antagonists/informants: a company that may have saved millions of lives through modification tech, and also plausibly monopolizes, gatekeeps, or profits unevenly from who gets to survive well.

## Why Aveline Is Really Here

The SEZAC equity stake is the official reason for Aveline's presence, but the real draw is that Saigon SEZ has become a **regulatory frontier for biological research** — work that wouldn't clear ethics review back home, enabled by a corpo-gov more interested in outcomes than oversight and a deep pool of desperate, undocumented labor willing to participate for money or medical access. Aveline is the most prominent _legitimate_ face of this research economy, not its only one. See [[Population and Demographics]] for how this reshapes the zone's foreign population well beyond just corporate leadership.
