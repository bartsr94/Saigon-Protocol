# Kaien Sōgō

**Type:** Sōgō shōsha (general trading house) 
**Origin:** Japan 
**Governance:** Council of division heads (keiretsu-style, collective/professional-manager control) 
**Role:** Senior partner in [[The Compact]]

## Overview

Kaien Sōgō is structured in the mold of the real historical _sōgō shōsha_ — sprawling general trading houses (the same category as firms like Mitsubishi Corp or Mitsui) with hands in logistics, resource extraction, and infrastructure all at once. Kaien's stake in Saigon SEZ runs through exactly that lineage: a trading house's natural interest in controlling a chokepoint city.

## Governance

Unlike a family-controlled chaebol, Kaien answers to a **Council** — the heads of its core divisions, meeting to steer strategy collectively. This makes Kaien:

- Cautious and institutional
- Inclined to play long games
- Prone to prioritizing legal cover and plausible deniability over speed

In [[The Compact]], Kaien is the half most likely to insist a thing be done "properly" — which in practice means slowly, and means there's always a paper trail leading somewhere else.
