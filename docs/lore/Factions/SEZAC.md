# SEZAC — Saigon Special Economic Zone Administrative Committee

**Type:** Nominal government / administrative body 
**Real power:** Low — functions as legal cover for [[The Compact]] 
**Status:** Active, uncontested

## Overview

SEZAC is the zone's government in every formal sense: a Chairperson, an official seal, a published charter, a permitting bureaucracy. It is the direct legal descendant of the old Vietnamese state's last real act of sovereignty — inviting in "anyone willing to help" as the climate crisis outpaced any single nation's ability to cope.

Almost no one who actually lives in Saigon believes SEZAC governs anything. Its board seats are held by the corporate stakeholders themselves — chiefly [[Kaien Sogo]] and [[Baekho Group]], with a minority presence from [[Aveline Biogenetics]] and others. SEZAC functions less as an authority and more as the paperwork through which those stakeholders' decisions become law after the fact.

## Function in Play

Official channels through SEZAC exist and produce real, legally binding documents — but they almost never point at where a decision actually got made. This is a standing source of narrative friction: a detective following the paper trail through SEZAC will find process, precedent, and dead ends, while the actual decision-makers sit one level up.
