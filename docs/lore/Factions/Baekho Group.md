# Baekho Group

**Type:** Chaebol (family-controlled conglomerate) 
**Origin:** Korea 
**Governance:** Founding family, via circular cross-shareholding 
**Role:** Junior-in-name, aggressive-in-practice partner in [[The Compact]]

## Overview

"Baekho" means white tiger. The Group is a chaebol in the classic mold: a founding family retains control of the whole conglomerate through a web of circular cross-shareholding, despite formally owning only a fraction of it outright — the same structure that lets real-world chaebol families like those behind Samsung or Hyundai run empires many times the size of their direct stake.

## Governance

Centralized and dynastic rather than collective. This makes Baekho:

- Faster-moving than [[Kaien Sogo]]
- More aggressive
- Comfortable operating right at the edge of what [[SEZAC]]'s charter technically permits

In [[The Compact]], Baekho is the half most likely to have already done the thing before Kaien finishes discussing whether it should be done.

## Security Arm

Baekho supplies a significant share of the officer corps embedded in [[Nusantara Diaspora Resettlement and Workforce Solutions]] — seconded advisors commanding Nusantara's rank-and-file alongside Kaien-sourced counterparts. This keeps Baekho's fingerprints on the Compact's rougher operations without the company's own name attached.
