# The Compact

**Type:** Joint venture (informal name) 
**Members:** [[Kaien Sogo]] (Japanese) + [[Baekho Group]] (Korean) 
**Real power:** Dominant — the actual government of Saigon SEZ 
**Status:** Active, internally divided

## Overview

The dominant power in Saigon SEZ. "The Compact" is not the joint venture's legal name — it's what everyone in the city calls it regardless, corporate paperwork or not. Formally, it operates through controlling board seats on [[SEZAC]], which supplies the legal shell.

## Internal Structure & Friction

The Compact is a marriage between two fundamentally different governance philosophies, and the friction between them is structural, not personal:

- **[[Kaien Sogo]]** — a _sōgō shōsha_-style general trading house, governed collectively by a Council of division heads. Cautious, institutional, plays long games, prizes legal cover and plausible deniability. Insists things be done "properly" — meaning slowly, and meaning there's always a paper trail leading somewhere else.
- **[[Baekho Group]]** — a chaebol controlled by a founding family through circular cross-shareholding. Faster-moving, more aggressive, comfortable operating at the edge of what SEZAC's charter technically permits. Has often already done the thing before Kaien finishes discussing whether it should be done.

Both halves must co-sign anything of consequence. Genuine deadlocks happen.

## Function in Play

A case that snags on _which half of the Compact actually ordered this_ is one of the most natural story engines available in Saigon Protocol. The Compact isn't a single villain — it's a marriage, and marriages have secrets from each other. Player-facing plots can exploit the seam between the two halves: a Kaien-aligned lead who wants something handled quietly, a Baekho-aligned lead who already handled it loudly.

The Compact's shared muscle is contracted out rather than run in-house by either half — see [[Nusantara Diaspora Resettlement and Workforce Solutions]].
