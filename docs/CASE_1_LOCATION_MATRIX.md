# Case 1 Location Matrix

*Working location matrix for the Aveline breakout investigation. This document translates the current case story, overworld plan, and cast spec into playable places.*

## Goal

Define the core locations required for Case 1 and clarify:

- which districts they belong to
- what story job they do
- which characters are encountered there
- what clues or reveals they support
- what art / ambience they require
- whether they are mandatory or optional

This document is meant to bridge narrative design and implementation planning. It is not final prose content.

## Top-level structure

Case 1 should be organized around four districts:

- **District 4**: Aveline pressure zone, flood-wall edge, opening lie
- **District 1**: official channels, records, corporate insulation
- **District 5 / Cholon**: witnesses, gray medicine, memory, rumor, paper
- **District 2**: sacrificed zone, collapse of the official story, final confrontation

The investigation should move across all four, but not necessarily in one rigid line. Some discoveries in one district should unlock or enrich scenes in another.

## Location design principles

- Every location should do at least two jobs: atmosphere plus investigation, or character plus clue, etc.
- The city should feel lived in; not every stop should be a plot room.
- Optional locations should still reveal something true about Saigon, even if they are not mandatory.
- Some locations should return changed after the player learns more.
- The player should gradually earn the right to access deeper and more controlled spaces.

## District 4

### 1. Aveline District 4 Laboratory Exterior

- **Type:** mandatory opening location
- **District:** 4
- **Story role:** first case scene; establishes false burglary narrative and official-channel unease
- **Primary characters:** Mei Hong, Sora Baek, responding uniforms
- **Core function:** introduces the lie and the pressure to accept it

#### What happens here

- player arrives from the intro
- learns Aveline has gone through official channels for an internal incident
- sees a tightly managed perimeter
- begins first interview with Mei Hong
- is denied full access to the inner lab

#### Clue value

- the security response feels wrong for a simple theft
- witnesses are careful, frightened, or rehearsed
- the company is searching for something more urgent than stolen property

#### Asset needs

- existing Aveline exterior background can remain
- ambient rain / industrial hush / flood-wall infrastructure

### 2. Aveline Intake / Front Lab Floor

- **Type:** mandatory early interior
- **District:** 4
- **Story role:** controlled access interior; gives the player just enough information to keep asking questions
- **Primary characters:** Mei Hong, Sora Baek, low-level staff
- **Core function:** let the player smell the cover-up without yet breaking it

#### What happens here

- sanitized walkthrough
- access to harmless or curated parts of the facility
- formal statement taking
- observation of staff behavior under pressure

#### Clue value

- operational inconsistencies
- evidence of rushed cleanup
- emotional tells from staff who know more than they can say

#### Asset needs

- generic lab interior or front-lab background
- controlled medical-industrial ambience

### 2a. Aveline Faculty Lounge

- **Type:** optional early sub-location, accessible from the start
- **District:** 4
- **Story role:** low-stakes staff-interview room off the front lab floor — where the detective can work the case sideways while CID's forensics pass on the main floor is still ongoing
- **Primary characters:** Lakshmi Avani, other unnamed on-duty Aveline staff (rotation-dependent, background texture)
- **Core function:** gives the player somewhere to go besides the two gatekept mandatory interviews (Mei Hong, Sora Baek) — rank-and-file staff who are less rehearsed, less powerful, and more willing to talk

#### Blurb

> The lounge is the one room in the lab that still smells like a workplace instead of a crime scene — cold coffee, a cracked vending machine, a wall of curling shift rosters. Half of Aveline's on-duty staff are parked in here waiting for CID to finish with the floor, trading nervous small talk and rehearsed non-answers in equal measure.

#### What happens here

- Mei Hong can point the player here directly in the first interview — most on-duty staff have been told to wait out the forensics pass in the lounge rather than loiter near the scene, which doubles as the in-fiction reason the detective has easy access to several witnesses at once
- informal, off-the-record-feeling conversations, in contrast to the controlled first interview
- first opportunity to interview Lakshmi Avani and (later, as more staff rotate through) other rank-and-file Aveline employees without going through Sora Baek's access gate

#### Clue value

- technical/scientific texture on the HN line from someone who isn't a primary suspect
- softer, less-rehearsed contradictions than Mei Hong's or Sora Baek's — the first sense that not everyone at Aveline is fully bought into the cover story
- lab-morale read: who's frightened, who's not, who's already talking about quitting

#### Asset needs

- lounge interior background (breakroom furniture, vending machine, shift-roster wall)
- low chatter / vending-machine hum ambience

### 3. Flood-Wall Service Road

- **Type:** early investigation location, mandatory or strongly recommended
- **District:** 4
- **Story role:** shows the lab in context of city infrastructure
- **Primary characters:** patrol officers, maintenance workers, possible first civilians affected by HN-12's movement
- **Core function:** move the case from sealed corporate interior into public space

#### What happens here

- interviews with people who saw unusual movement or heard panic
- environmental storytelling around wall systems and exterior exposure
- possibility of first ambiguous evidence of HN-12 outside containment

#### Clue value

- route evidence suggesting flight rather than theft extraction
- panic behavior that does not fit a planned industrial burglary
- early signs of food / water / chemical desperation

#### Asset needs

- flood-wall road background
- wind, rain, pumps, distant traffic

### 4. District 4 Public Incident Scene

- **Type:** later mandatory scene
- **District:** 4
- **Story role:** escalation point where the corporate fiction starts becoming unsustainable
- **Primary characters:** responding CID / uniforms, civilians, possibly Sora Baek arriving to manage the narrative
- **Core function:** convert suspicion into public stakes

#### What happens here

- one serious assault or eventual murder
- contradictory witness statements
- physical evidence too strange to fit a normal perpetrator cleanly

#### Clue value

- HN-12 is now clearly affecting the public sphere
- the player can compare the violence here to Aveline's earlier restraint and omissions
- enough pressure builds to justify demanding deeper lab access

#### Asset needs

- street-side incident background or multi-use District 4 exterior
- emergency-response ambience

### 5. Inner Containment Wing

- **Type:** gated mandatory reveal location
- **District:** 4
- **Story role:** major forensic reveal; proves breakout, not break-in
- **Primary characters:** possibly Mei Hong, Sora Baek, Tomas Varga
- **Core function:** the room testifies against the lie

#### Access condition

- player should only get in after gathering enough leverage, contradictions, or public heat

#### What happens here

- detailed scene reading
- damage pattern analysis
- containment / restraint evidence
- direct signs of staged intrusion

#### Clue value

- breach came from inside
- subject was alive and physically contained
- cover story was built after the event

#### Asset needs

- new containment corridor / chamber background
- uncomfortable low mechanical ambience

## District 1

### 6. CID Office

- **Type:** recurring mandatory hub
- **District:** 1
- **Story role:** investigation home base and official processing point
- **Primary characters:** CID superior or coworker, player-facing administrative friction
- **Core function:** grounds the detective in real police work and reminds the player what official process looks like

#### What happens here

- debriefs
- evidence review
- pressure from above
- case framing discussions

#### Clue value

- how much room the detective still has inside official channels
- whether the case is being quietly redirected

#### Asset needs

- CID office background
- climate-controlled administrative ambience

### 7. SEZAC Records / Licensing Office

- **Type:** mandatory or near-mandatory records location
- **District:** 1
- **Story role:** paper trail location
- **Primary characters:** Elian Morrow, records clerks, bureaucratic intermediaries
- **Core function:** reveal how official process launders harm

#### What happens here

- records requests
- licensing review
- denied or partial access
- discovery of ghost identities / emergency authorizations

#### Clue value

- Aveline's work was framed as legal necessity
- transit, trial, and death categories were made to survive inquiry

#### Asset needs

- records archive / licensing office background
- sterile office ambience

### 8. District 1 Corporate Plaza / Meeting Space

- **Type:** optional or later mandatory confrontation space
- **District:** 1
- **Story role:** polished public face of elite power
- **Primary characters:** Lucien Vale in formal mode, possibly Sora Baek
- **Core function:** let the player see how far the case reaches above the lab floor

#### What happens here

- formal interview
- quiet intimidation
- ideological argument about adaptation, necessity, and scale

#### Clue value

- the project was not a hidden personal obsession
- high-level actors see the work as tragic but acceptable

#### Asset needs

- corporate lobby / private lounge / elevated transit concourse
- refined interior ambience

## District 5 / Cholon

### 9. Gray Clinic

- **Type:** mandatory mid-case location
- **District:** 5
- **Story role:** medical underbelly of the city
- **Primary characters:** clinic operator, nurses, patients, maybe trace links to Nhi Quyen
- **Core function:** connect Aveline's official work to off-book care and aftercare

#### What happens here

- player follows medical, chemical, or subject-support leads
- sees who falls through official systems
- learns something about HN-12's bodily needs

#### Clue value

- support compounds or symptom management
- evidence that subjects or near-subjects were routed through unofficial treatment
- hints of Nusantara-linked movement

#### Asset needs

- clinic interior background
- muffled crowd / fan / refrigeration ambience

### 10. Informant Row / Tea House / Street Stall

- **Type:** recurring social location
- **District:** 5
- **Story role:** rumor, memory, contacts, street intelligence
- **Primary characters:** Cholon informant, small-time brokers, witnesses
- **Core function:** let the player earn knowledge socially rather than procedurally

#### What happens here

- rumor gathering
- favor trading
- optional side interactions that may pay back later

#### Clue value

- who left Aveline recently
- where Nhi or Tomas may have gone
- how the city is already talking about the strange incidents

#### Asset needs

- dense Cholon street or tea-house background
- crowd / cookware / layered languages ambience

### 11. Former Employee Safe Flat

- **Type:** mid-case witness location
- **District:** 5
- **Story role:** memory and fallout
- **Primary characters:** former Aveline employee, possibly Tomas or another ex-worker
- **Core function:** expose what people knew before the breach

#### What happens here

- interview with burned-out or frightened former worker
- discovery of small physical records, messages, or testimonies
- possible link to Nhi Quyen

#### Clue value

- instability was known before HN-12 escaped
- prior subject deaths were not mere bad luck
- low-level staff saw enough to become afraid

#### Asset needs

- modest apartment / safe-flat background
- rain-through-windows / domestic-city ambience

### 12. Paper Broker / Records Intermediary

- **Type:** optional but valuable
- **District:** 5
- **Story role:** identity and disappearance seam
- **Primary characters:** fixer, paper broker, forged-record specialist
- **Core function:** translate bureaucratic abstraction into street reality

#### What happens here

- player learns how people vanish on paper
- may cross-reference Nusantara intake and SEZAC records
- may unlock cleaner understanding of ghost identities

#### Clue value

- how HN subjects were renamed, lost, or buried administratively
- how refugee transit can be made invisible

#### Asset needs

- cramped back-office or market-side records den
- paper, fan, market spillover ambience

## District 2

### 13. Flooded Access Route

- **Type:** late mandatory approach location
- **District:** 2
- **Story role:** psychological transition out of the defended city
- **Primary characters:** patrol contact, scavengers, maybe earlier side characters reappearing
- **Core function:** show the physical cost of the city's sacrificed zones

#### What happens here

- navigation into increasingly unstable territory
- environmental hazard emphasis
- possibility of recent HN-12 trace discovery

#### Clue value

- HN-12 moved where ordinary bodies do not move safely
- their adaptation and suffering are both visible in the route they took

#### Asset needs

- flooded roadway / elevated ruin background
- exposed wind / water / distant structure ambience

### 14. Reclaimed Marsh Edge / Service Tunnel

- **Type:** late investigation node
- **District:** 2
- **Story role:** threshold space between pursuit and confrontation
- **Primary characters:** few or none; mostly environmental storytelling
- **Core function:** let the city itself carry some of the dread

#### What happens here

- tracking, searching, piecing together the subject's recent state
- evidence of attempts at shelter, water, chemical scavenging, or self-preservation

#### Clue value

- HN-12 is surviving, not hunting
- the subject still thinks in fragments of need and memory

#### Asset needs

- marsh edge or tunnel background
- sparse, lonely ambience

### 15. Drowned Tower / Final Encounter Space

- **Type:** mandatory climax
- **District:** 2
- **Story role:** lucid confrontation with HN-12
- **Primary characters:** HN-12, possibly Nhi Quyen depending on final structure
- **Core function:** transform the case from institutional truth into human tragedy

#### What happens here

- player meets HN-12 in one of their lucid windows
- learns enough of the person behind the code
- is forced into final moral or tactical choice

#### Clue / story value

- final confirmation of HN-12's humanity
- emotional truth of what Aveline did
- culmination of adaptation-horror theme in a sacrificed zone

#### Asset needs

- major final background
- unique ambience treatment
- possible portrait treatment if HN-12 is rendered as a speaking NPC

## Optional city-life locations

These are not strictly required to solve Case 1, but they help the world feel larger than the plot and can support side stories.

### 16. Worker Canteen / Night Stall

- **District:** 4 or 5
- **Purpose:** side stories, casual witness conversations, district texture
- **Value:** lets players hear how normal people talk about the city, Aveline, and Nusantara

### 17. District Transit Platform

- **District:** 1 or 4
- **Purpose:** travel framing, class contrast, chance encounters
- **Value:** reinforces the city's vertical and infrastructural life

### 18. Nusantara Intake / Housing Block

- **District:** 4-adjacent or 5-adjacent depending final geography
- **Purpose:** later casefile and side-story location
- **Value:** makes the refugee pipeline physically real

This location may become more important once the Nusantara supporting cast is fully defined.

## Recurrence plan

Some locations should recur with changed meaning:

- **Aveline lab exterior**: first as controlled mystery, later as a site under pressure
- **CID office**: first as routine home base, later as a place where the case is slipping beyond normal procedure
- **Cholon social space**: first as rumor market, later as a place where the player returns with sharper questions
- **District 2 approach**: first inaccessible or irrelevant, later inevitable
